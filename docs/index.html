The deposit that remembers the dent was already there.

At a Bali scooter counter the jaminan, the deposit, is often your passport, and at return whoever holds it wins the argument. Jaminan holds the deposit on PayPal instead: when the shop points at a mark, it cannot be charged if the confirmed pickup baseline already lists that damage there or, once a model passes the bench, the vision model finds it in the pickup photos, and the deposit pays only the lines the renter accepts.

No. 1 Aturan · the rule

The owner points. The shared baseline answers.

At pickup, renter and shop walk eight angles round the scooter and confirm the photos together. At return the shop taps each mark it wants to charge and names it from the published tariff. A vision model answers narrow questions about the photos both sides confirmed. It never sets a price.

  1. 1Toko menunjukThe shop taps a mark and picks its part and kind from the tariff list.mirror_housing · crack · $18.00
  2. 2Foto awal menjawabThe model only answers what the shared pickup photos can show.visible_at_pickup · pickup_comparable · mark_at_return
  3. 3Penyewa memutuskanThe renter accepts or contests each line. The hold pays only what they accept.capture · final_capture=true

What the shared baseline can't show, nobody can charge.

Code turns the model's answers into one of these lines, in this order. Only the last kind of line has a price, and only from the shop's published tariff.

Who it is for: family-run scooter counters in Jakarta and Bali, and the tourists and locals who rent from them and today leave a passport or KTP as the deposit.

No. 1½ Coba · your turn

Tap a claim. See what the model said.

AI-generated test images (not photographs) A pickup image made by qwen-image-2.0 and a return image edited from it by wan2.6-image, which removed both mirrors. The builder pointed two claims; a script running the same crop, model-call and decision steps as the app's claim path asked the vision model three times each. One angle, no rental, no close-ups.

Tap a box on the return image. The answer is the model's recorded output for that spot.

These are recorded answers, not a benchmark and not real-world evidence. No rental or payment was involved. Had this model been pinned without the bench, the rules would have priced C1 at the tariff's dent price, $45.00, for a scuff that was there at pickup. A false “new” on an old mark is the error the benchmark has to rule out before any model is pinned, so no model can price a line until it passes the bench on real photos.

No. 2 Uang · the money

One authorization carries the whole rental.

One renter approval authorizes the fee and the deposit together ($115.00) and saves the wallet for later fines. Everything after happens on that one authorization, and every stamp waits for PayPal's own read-back.

authorize $115.00→Created→ capture fee $15.00 · final_capture=false→Partially captured
accepted line $18.00 · final_capture=true→Captured→capture again→422 refused
no new damage · void→Voided
contested · not paid from the hold · void · invoice $18.00 sent→Voided

Rentals longer than three days are reauthorized once on day 4, inside PayPal's day 4 to 29 window. On the sandbox, reauthorize answered 201 on two holds, one with its fee already captured, and refused a second reauthorize of an already reauthorized hold with 422 REAUTHORIZATION_TOO_SOON. If PayPal refuses one, the service authorizes again on the saved wallet and voids the old hold. Inside the app, the day-4 trigger rule and this fallback are unit-tested (the daily cron that runs them is not); the recorded sandbox calls are the live proof. evidence/paypal-reauthorize.jsonl

No. 4 Cara kerja · how it is built

Arithmetic says no first. PayPal says yes last.

Bad frames never reach the model

Blur and glare are rejected by arithmetic on a fixed 1280 px frame, before any model call.

The model answers, never prices

Per tap: is there a mark now, is it the kind named, was it visible at pickup, can the pickup photo show that spot.

Qwen VL · JSON schema · 0–1000 boxes

Code decides

The answers become a line in a fixed order. A model the benchmark has not pinned can never produce a chargeable line.

rules.py · decide_claim

The handover is written to PayPal

PayPal's shipment tracking on the fee capture is the handover record: its tracking number carries the first 8 hex characters of the confirmed baseline's SHA-256.

orders/{id}/track

Fines later, on the saved wallet

A traffic-camera ticket whose plate and time match the rental is charged to the saved wallet, capped by the ceiling for that violation; the renter has 72 hours to contest. Offline-tested only: the recorded runs prove a merchant-initiated authorize on a saved wallet, not a fine.

No. 5 Batas · honest by design

Fair questions a judge would ask.

What does the AI decide?

Nothing about money. For each mark the shop taps, the vision model answers narrow questions about the shared pickup photos: is there a mark now, is it the kind named, was it visible at pickup, can the pickup photo show that spot. Code turns the answers into “cannot be charged” or a line priced from the tariff.

Has the vision model been benchmarked?

Not yet: the bench on real scooter photos has not run. Until a model is pinned by it, every live claim ends “comparison unavailable — nothing charged from the hold”, which is why the recorded settlements on this page use scripted answers. The “Tap a claim” section shows the model's recorded answers on AI-generated test images (not photographs); it read both claims wrong, which is a reason for the bench, not a measurement. No accuracy number appears anywhere.

What stops the shop from charging an old dent?

Two locks. The pickup baseline both sides confirmed: a mark it shows, or a spot it cannot show, cannot be charged. And the renter: the hold pays only the lines they accept.

And if the renter contests everything?

Every contested line leaves as an itemized PayPal invoice at the published tariff price, with a link to the evidence pack, and the hold is released. That is the trade: the hold never pays a line the renter did not accept, and the shop keeps a priced claim on photos both sides confirmed.

Can an AI agent book a scooter?

Yes, through four MCP tools: list_vehicles, quote_rental, start_rental and get_rental. The agent can price a rental and ask for it, but start_rental returns a PayPal link that only the renter can approve. No tool approves, answers a damage line, captures, voids or refunds. Claude Code and Codex each booked one on the sandbox on 2026-10-24 and read back the authorization; the transcripts are in evidence/mcp.

What happens when I open /judge?

It mints a fresh sandbox rental on a saved sandbox wallet, with no login, and opens it on the shop's phone; today it can only release the hold (read back VOIDED). A full-path button runs PayPal's real approval page with a sandbox buyer.
